# GoTRVL Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

GoTRVL ("we", "the app") is a travel companion app for exploring the
Philippines. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and
your choices.

## Data we collect

- **Account data**: name, email address, Firebase user ID, and your password
  (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it in plain text).
- **Profile data**: display name, age, location, bio, and photos you choose to
  add.
- **Travel activity**: places you mark as visited, points and level, posts,
  likes, comments, buddy matches, and chat messages.
- **LIVE streams**: stream titles, scheduling metadata, and chat messages sent
  during live streams.
- **Business listings**: if you submit a business listing, we collect the
  business contact info you provide (business name, category, phone number,
  website, and social links) to publish the listing in the app.
- **Admin access**: admin email addresses used to gate the hidden admin
  console, together with a role check on our backend.
- **Location**: your device location is used only to show your position on the
  map and to open directions. It is never uploaded to our servers, and the app
  works without granting location permission.
- **Photos**: images you attach to posts or your profile are stored in Firebase
  Storage so they can be displayed in the app.

## How we use it

We use your data solely to operate the app: authentication, your travel
profile and gamification, the social feed, matching, messaging, live streams,
and business listings. We do not sell your personal data.

## Ads

The app shows house and promotional slots only. No third-party advertising SDK
is bundled, and we collect no data for advertising purposes.

## Affiliate links

When enabled, partner buttons (for example stays and activities) open the
partner's website in your browser. We share no personal data with affiliates.
Any data collected on a partner website is governed by that partner's own
privacy policy.

## Data sharing

- Infrastructure is provided by **Google Firebase** (Authentication,
  Firestore, Storage) and **Google Maps**, subject to Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Use of Google Maps is also subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service:
  https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/
- We share no personal data with other third parties.

## Account deletion and data retention

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: **Profile -> Settings
-> Delete Account**. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts,
photos, and messages from our systems.

We keep your data only as long as needed to operate the app: account data for
as long as your account is active, user content until you delete it, and
business listings until they are removed. Residual copies in backups expire on
Google's standard retention schedule.

## Your rights

You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us
at the support email below.

## Children
