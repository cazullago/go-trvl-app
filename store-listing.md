## Full description
**GoTRVL turns traveling the Philippines into a game.**

🗺️ **PLAYFUL MAP OF THE PHILIPPINES**
Search 18 hand-picked destinations — from Siargao's surf to Bohol's Chocolate
Hills — on a custom bohemian-styled map. Filter pins by Where to Go, Things to
Do, Where to Eat, Where to Stay and Where to Buy. Tap any pin for details,
prices and one-tap directions.

⭐ **EARN POINTS, CLIMB LEVELS**
Mark places as done when you visit and earn points for every island you
explore. Rise from New Traveler all the way to Legendary Traveler, collect
passport stamps and compete on the Island Leaderboard.

📸 **SHARE YOUR ADVENTURES**
Post photos, tag locations, like and comment on fellow travelers' journeys.

💌 **FIND YOUR TRAVEL BUDDY**
Swipe to match with travelers heading where you're heading, then chat and plan
trips together.

🪪 **YOUR TRAVEL PASSPORT**
A passport-style profile with your level, stats, adventures and stamps.

Pack your bags — the Philippines is waiting. Download GoTRVL and start
collecting islands! 🌺
